06 · Good to know

Is this for you?

A quick, honest look at who gets the most out of Start With Kunmi, and the questions people ask before they apply.

This is for you if

You want to do the work

  • You want to build and make things, not just consume content about them.
  • You are happy to share unfinished work and take honest feedback.
  • You want to turn interest into something you can actually do.
  • You can show up to a weekly rhythm and keep going.

This is not for you if

You want a shortcut

  • You are looking for certificates for their own sake.
  • You want to collect resources without ever using them.
  • You would rather watch than do.
  • You want a chat group to perform ambition in.
Do I need experience to join?

No. You do not need everything figured out. You need somewhere to begin, and people who take the beginning seriously. Start where you are.

What will I actually do here?

You will take part in weekly challenges, share work in progress, get honest feedback, and read resources that are curated rather than dumped. The aim is to make progress visible, for you and for everyone around you.

How much time does it take?

The community runs on a weekly rhythm of sessions, prompts and conversations. Take what fits your week. Progress comes from doing the work, so the more you put in, the more you get out.

How does joining work?

Tap Apply and fill in the short form. This is a small, intentional space rather than an open mailing list, so applications are reviewed before members are added.

Is this a course?

No. Nobody is handing out certificates here. The goal is being able to do something with what you know, and that happens through practice and feedback on real work.
