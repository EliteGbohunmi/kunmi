01 / ABOUT KUNMI

MORE THAN
A NAME.

Kunmi is a creative builder whose work sits at the intersection of craft, storytelling and community. She believes the most meaningful things are built slowly, with intention, and in service of something bigger than any one person.

She started Zynora because building alone is overrated — and because the people you build with matter as much as the work itself.

04 / BEYOND THE WORK
Kunmi the Great

BEYOND
THE TITLES.

Kunmi is driven by a simple belief: that the best work comes from honesty, curiosity and care. She's interested in the details, the quiet moments, and the things that don't make it onto a résumé.

When she's not building, she's thinking about what it means to live and work with intention — and how to bring more of that into everything she does.
